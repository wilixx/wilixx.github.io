Presented our work on **Pattern-Aware Virtual Network Embedding Optimization for Cloud Data Centers** at IEEE PIMRC 2026 in Singapore. The conference was also an opportunity to exchange ideas and learn from fellow researchers.

The work studies how complementary resource demands can be matched to improve online virtual network embedding in cloud data centers.
