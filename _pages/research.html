Research科学研究

Satellite Networking, Green Computing, AI, Graph Theory卫星网络、绿色计算、人工智能、图论

My main focus is mega-LEO satellite networking: designing simple, effective algorithms for the evolving needs of users and network operators in the 6G and AI era. I aim for methods that could be evaluated and adapted for constellations such as Starlink and Qianfan, with the longer-term goal of supporting useful, widely accessible services. My broader interests include graph theory, the P versus NP question, green computing for AI, and applications of AI to cryptography, biology and medicine.

我的主要研究方向是巨型 LEO 卫星互联网组网。立足 6G 与人工智能时代通信基础设施领域的关键技术需求与瓶颈，开展卫星组网、绿色计算与人工智能方向的研究，设计简单、高效、可用、易实现、易部署的技术方法，推动其在 Starlink、千帆等巨型星座中的应用，让卫星通信连接全球、走进千家万户，服务于人们的日常生活。同时关注图论、P 与 NP 这一世界难题、面向人工智能的绿色计算，以及人工智能在密码学、生命科学与医学中的应用。

I use mathematical optimization, graph theory, geometry and experiments to identify useful structure, develop algorithms and test new ideas. I hope to contribute rigorous research that is simple in design, effective in practice and useful beyond the laboratory.

研究中结合数学优化、图论、几何学与实验科学，从问题中发现结构与规律，形成并检验新的方法。希望研究简明扎实，从实验室走向广泛的实际应用。

I welcome self-motivated students and collaborators who enjoy patient, careful work on concrete questions, as well as discussions and joint projects with industry teams addressing practical problems. Contact: bqguo@stu.xidian.edu.cn.

欢迎有内在动力、愿意耐心钻研的同学与同行交流合作，也欢迎产业界围绕实际需求开展项目合作。邮箱：bqguo@stu.xidian.edu.cn。

Research interests研究兴趣

Networking for Mega-LEO constellations巨型低轨卫星星座组网

I study networking algorithm design for large, moving constellations: routing, resilience, semantic communication and in-orbit computing. The aim is to meet service requirements with manageable implementation costs. Related work has appeared in IEEE TMC, Wireless Communications, TCOM and TVT, and at several conferences.

围绕大规模动态星座设计组网算法，涵盖路由、韧性、语义通信与星上计算，关注业务需求与实现成本之间的平衡。相关研究发表于 IEEE TMC、Wireless Communications、TCOM、TVT 及多个学术会议。

Green computing for AI面向人工智能的绿色计算

Resource packing in data centers, workflow scheduling and multi-step language-model inference. I am interested in using fewer computing resources to meet a given service requirement, and in understanding the trade-offs between energy, latency and utilization.

关注数据中心资源装箱、工作流调度与多步骤大模型推理调度，研究如何以更少的计算资源满足业务需求，以及能耗、时延和资源利用率之间的权衡。

Whether P=NPP 是否等于 NP

An exploratory interest in computational complexity, grounded in specific graph and NP-hard scheduling problems. I study exact and approximate methods, and how AI and mathematical optimization may help reveal useful problem structure.

对计算复杂性的探索兴趣，从具体图问题与 NP 难调度问题出发，研究精确与近似方法，并探索人工智能和数学优化如何帮助发现可利用的问题结构。

AI for cryptography人工智能与密码学

Exploring how learning-based methods can support the analysis and design of cryptographic systems, with attention to mathematical guarantees and reproducible evaluation.

探索学习方法如何辅助密码系统的分析与设计，重视数学保证和可复现的评估。

Natural and life sciences自然科学与生命科学

Curiosity also leads me to questions in the natural and life sciences. I enjoy learning across fields and exploring how new observations can inform useful models and methods.

出于好奇心，持续关注自然科学与生命科学中的问题，从不同领域的新发现中学习，探索有用的模型与方法。
